A home for your MeshCore mesh.

Find your local MeshCore organization, set up your companion with the right settings and channels, and check in to nets. When you're ready to bring a repeater, your organization can help keep it properly configured.

Check in with your companion

Get the right settings for your area

Each organization has a Guide with its radio settings and channels. Scan a QR code to add a channel instead of typing in its key. Anyone can read the Guide, so you can look before you join. Members also get the organization's private channels.

{{template "landing-preview-guide"}}

For repeater owners

{{template "need-tag" "usb"}}

Set up a new repeater in one step

Plug a freshly flashed repeater into your computer and pick one of your organization's configuration profiles. MeshTender sets the radio, name, region and the rest of the profile for you, so you don't have to type any commands.

{{template "landing-preview-setup"}}
{{template "need-tag" "none"}}

Share access without sharing passwords

Keep notes and a maintenance log for each repeater. Let other people run commands on it, whether that's one person you invite, a trusted steward, or your whole organization, and choose which commands each can use. They connect over the air with their own KISS modem, so you don't need one.

If you do have a KISS modem, you can manage your own repeaters over the air too.

{{template "landing-preview-sharing"}}

For organization admins

{{template "need-tag" "none"}}

Configure every repeater the same way

Publish your organization's radio settings and configuration profiles, and draw your regions on a map. Repeater owners see exactly which commands to run for their location, and new repeaters get them automatically during setup.

{{template "landing-preview-config"}}
{{template "need-tag" "modem"}}

Manage your repeaters over the air

Plug a MeshCore radio running KISS modem firmware into your computer, and you can run commands on any repeater you've been given access to, right from your browser. No other MeshCore tool does this.

  • {{template "icon-check" ""}}Run a net over the air, with announcements and acknowledgments sent automatically
  • {{template "icon-check" ""}}Look back at every command sent and the reply it got
