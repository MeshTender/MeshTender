A home for your MeshCore mesh.

Your organization's repeaters, settings and weekly nets in one place. Members check in with the companion app they already use, repeater owners set up to the organization's spec, and operators with a KISS modem can manage repeaters over the air.

For members

{{template "landing-tag" "companion"}}

Check in from the app you already use

Register the companion you carry, then check in to your organization's weekly net by sending a flood advert. Follow the roster and the news as it comes in, and read your organization's docs and recommended setup any time.

{{template "landing-preview-schedule"}}

For repeater owners

{{template "landing-tag" "usb"}}

Set up a new repeater to your organization's spec

Plug a freshly flashed repeater into your computer and pick your organization's profile. MeshTender writes the radio, name, region for where it stands and settings in one pass, like the flasher's Repeater Setup, without typing any of it.

{{template "landing-preview-setup"}}
{{template "landing-tag" "none"}}

Document it, and share it without passwords

Keep each repeater's notes and maintenance history, publish a public page with its stewards, and decide who may run which commands on it: a single-use invite, a trusted steward, or your organization.

{{template "landing-preview-sharing"}}

For organizations

{{template "landing-tag" "none"}}

One configuration for the whole region

Publish your radio settings and configuration profiles, and draw regions on a map. Members see the exact commands for where their repeater stands, and new repeaters get them at setup.

{{template "landing-preview-config"}}

With a KISS modem

{{template "landing-tag" "modem"}}

Manage repeaters over the air

For admins, stewards and net control: plug a MeshCore radio flashed as a KISS modem into your computer to run CLI commands on repeaters from the browser, confirm they're reachable, apply your organization's configuration, and run a net on the air. Every command and reply is logged.
