{{$step}} · Register your companion Optional

You don't need to register to use the mesh. Registering lets MeshTender count your adverts as check-ins for nets. Copy its public key from the app and add it under Companions, then send a flood advert within 24 hours: once an observer hears it, the companion is confirmed. A registration that's never heard expires.

Register a companion

{{$checkIn}} · Check in during a net

While a net is in session, send a flood advert from any companion you've registered. That's your check-in.

  • Your companion's clock must be within ten minutes; the app sets it from your phone.
  • A zero-hop advert counts, but only observers in direct range hear it.
